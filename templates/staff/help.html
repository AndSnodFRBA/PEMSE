1. Logging In
  1. 1Go to www.panhandleems.com and click Staff Sign In (or go to /staff/ directly).
  2. 2Enter your username and password, then click Log In.
Lost password? Email emseducation19@gmail.com. Staff accounts are created by an admin — contact Andrew to set up new staff logins.
2. Staff Dashboard

The dashboard shows all active students across courses, with filters for status, document issues, and balance flags.

  1. 1Use the filter bar to narrow students by course, status (Active/Withdrawn), or document flags.
  2. 2Click any student row to open their Student Detail page.
  3. 3The sidebar gives quick access to all staff functions.
3. Managing Students
  1. 1From the dashboard, click a student's name to open their record.
  2. 2You can view attendance history, documents, grades, payments, and notes.
  3. 3To edit a student's info, click Edit Student on their detail page.
  4. 4To change enrollment status, use the Assign Course or Deactivate buttons.
Staff notes added to a student's record are not visible to the student. Use these for internal tracking only.
4. Inviting New Students
  1. 1Go to Courses → Course Detail for the relevant course.
  2. 2Click Invite Student and enter their email address.
  3. 3The student receives an email with a secure registration link.
You can resend or cancel pending invitations from the same course detail page.
5. Courses
  1. 1Go to Courses → Course List to see all courses.
  2. 2Click Add Course to create a new course (sets term dates, type, instructor).
  3. 3From a course detail page you can assign instructors and send evaluations.
6. Attendance Log
  1. 1Go to Compliance → Attendance Log.
  2. 2Filter by course or date range to view session records.
  3. 3Click a session to see individual student status and export as PDF or CSV.
7. Documents Queue
  1. 1Go to Compliance → Document Queue.
  2. 2Review and approve or reject submitted student documents.
Students are notified automatically when documents are approved or rejected.
8. Handbook & Content
  1. 1Go to Content → Handbook Chapters to edit the student handbook.
  2. 2Go to Reminders to schedule or send bulk notifications to students.
  3. 3Go to Settings → Site Settings to update agency info, logo, and contact details.
