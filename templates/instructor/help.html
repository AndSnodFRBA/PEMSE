1. Logging In
  1. 1Go to www.panhandleems.com and click Sign In.
  2. 2Enter your username and password, then click Log In.
  3. 3First login? You'll be prompted to update your profile.
Lost password? Use the Forgot password link or email emseducation19@gmail.com. Username is usually first initial + last name (e.g. jsmith).
2. Taking Attendance

Record within 48 hours of each session.

  1. 1Go to My Courses and click your course name.
  2. 2Click the Attendance tab.
  3. 3Choose the session date and session type (Lecture or Skills Lab).
  4. 4For each student, mark Present, Absent, or Late.
  5. 5Click Save Attendance.
Same-day lecture + lab? Submit attendance separately for each session type. You can return to edit an entry anytime — changes are logged.
3. Logging Your Hours
  1. 1Go to Log Hours in the sidebar.
  2. 2Click + Add Hours.
  3. 3Select the date, enter start & end time, and a description (e.g. "Lecture – airway management").
  4. 4Click Save.
Log hours within 7 days of each session. Your running total appears at the top of the Hours page.
4. Courses & Student Roster
  1. 1Click My Courses to see courses you're assigned to.
  2. 2Open a course to see enrolled students, their status, and retention flags.
A 🚩 red flag means the student is at risk per program criteria — contact admin. You cannot add or remove students; contact Robin for roster changes.
5. Calendar
  1. 1Click Calendar in the sidebar to view all scheduled sessions.
  2. 2Sessions are color-coded: Lecture (L), Skills Lab (S), Clinical (C).
  3. 3Use the iCal export icon to add the schedule to your personal calendar.
Calendar entries are managed by admin. Contact Robin to request a date or session change.
6. Updating Your Profile
  1. 1Click My Profile in the sidebar.
  2. 2Update your phone, email, or password as needed.
  3. 3Click Save Changes.
7. Observations & Meetings

When a director observes your instruction or schedules a check-in, it appears here.

  1. 1Click Observations or Meetings & Reviews.
  2. 2Review the note, then click Acknowledge to confirm you've seen it.
