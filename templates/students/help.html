1. Getting Started
  1. 1Check your email for your invitation link from PEMSE. Click the link to set up your account.
  2. 2Create a password and fill in your profile information.
  3. 3After setup, log in at www.panhandleems.com with your email and password.
Forgot your password? Use the Forgot password link on the login page or email emseducation19@gmail.com.
2. Your Dashboard

Your dashboard shows what needs attention. Items flagged with ! require action.

  1. 1Registration — complete and submit your program registration form.
  2. 2Handbook — read and digitally sign the student handbook.
  3. 3Upcoming sessions — your next scheduled class days.
  4. 4Notifications — alerts about deadlines, documents, and program updates.
3. Registration Form
  1. 1Click Registration in the sidebar.
  2. 2Fill in all sections: personal info, emergency contacts, and medical information.
  3. 3Review and click Submit Registration.
Your registration must be submitted before program activities can begin. Admin will notify you once it's reviewed.
4. Student Handbook
  1. 1Click Handbook in the sidebar.
  2. 2Read through all chapters — you must complete each section.
  3. 3Click Sign Handbook at the bottom to confirm you've read it.
The handbook is updated periodically. You'll be notified if you need to re-sign after an update.
5. My Documents
  1. 1Click My Documents in the sidebar.
  2. 2Upload required documents (CPR card, immunizations, etc.) by clicking Upload next to each item.
  3. 3Documents show as Approved, Rejected, or Pending Review.
If a document is rejected, you'll see a reason. Re-upload the corrected version and it will go back to admin for review.
6. My Grades
  1. 1Click My Grades in the sidebar.
  2. 2Your grades are shown by category: quizzes, exams, skills, worksheets, and participation.
  3. 3Your current overall grade and running average are displayed at the top.
Grades are entered by admin and instructors. Questions about a grade? Contact Andrew directly.
7. Class Calendar
  1. 1Click Calendar in the sidebar.
  2. 2Session types are color-coded: Lecture, Skills Lab, and Clinical.
  3. 3Use the iCal export to add the schedule to your phone or calendar app.
8. Clinical Rotations & Patient Contacts
  1. 1Click Clinical Rotations to log your clinical rotation hours and sites.
  2. 2Click Patient Contacts to log patient contacts during clinicals.
  3. 3Fill in the required fields and click Save.
Log patient contacts as soon as possible after each clinical — your instructor may review these.
9. Your Profile
  1. 1Click My Profile in the sidebar.
  2. 2Update your phone number, email address, or password.
  3. 3Click Save Changes.
Keep your contact info current — instructors and staff use it to reach you about schedule changes or absences.
